# Text Formatting

## Bold Text

For making text bold, add 2 stars on both sides.

**This is bold text**

---

## Italic Text

For making italic text, use 1 underscore or 1 star.

_This is italic text_

---

## Bold and Italic

You can use 3 stars or 3 underscores.

***This is bold and italic text***

___This is also bold and italic text___

*_This is also bold and italic text_*

---

## Strikethrough

~~This is crossed off text~~

---

## Highlight Text

==This text is highlighted==      
**This only works in some of the editors**

OR

<mark>Highlighted text</mark>

---

## Subscript

H<sub>2</sub>O

---

## Superscript

a<sup>2</sup> - b<sup>2</sup>
